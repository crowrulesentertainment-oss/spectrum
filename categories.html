The spectrum of achievement

Award Categories

A broad program designed to recognize excellence across entertainment, creativity, sport, technology and community.

Program preview. The divisions below describe the planned scope of the awards. The official category list for each annual season will be published by the awards administration before nominations open.
01 · ENTERTAINMENT

Film & Cinema

Motion pictures, independent film, acting, directing, writing and animation.

02 · ENTERTAINMENT

Music & Recording

Artists, albums, songs, groups, music videos and live performance.

03 · ENTERTAINMENT

Television & Streaming

Series, documentary, reality programming, hosting and streaming productions.

04 · DIGITAL

Digital Media & Podcasting

Podcasts, video creators, digital channels and independent media.

05 · CREATIVE

Arts & Creativity

Photography, visual arts, design, writing and creative projects.

06 · COMMUNITY

People & Community

Humanitarian work, volunteering, youth achievement and community impact.

07 · INNOVATION

Technology & Gaming

Technology, digital innovation, gaming and emerging creative tools.

08 · ATHLETICS

Sports & Athletics

Athletes, teams, coaching and sporting achievements.

09 · ENTERPRISE

Business & Entrepreneurship

Independent businesses, founders, leadership and innovation.

10 · WORLDWIDE

Global Culture & Achievement

Culture, education, literature, environment and international contributions.

Special honors

Potential special recognition includes Lifetime Achievement, Icon, Legacy, Humanitarian, Global Impact, Rising Star and Hall of Fame honors. These are separate from competitive categories and require their own published selection criteria.
