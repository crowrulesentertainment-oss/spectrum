About Spectrum Awards

A worldwide awards platform built to recognize talent in every form.

Our purpose

Spectrum Awards is an annual international recognition program presented by CrowRules Entertainment. Its purpose is to celebrate creative work, achievement, and the people and communities shaping culture around the world.

Every voice matters

The competitive awards program is founded on public participation: eligible public votes determine the winners. The program will publish its eligibility, nomination, voting, verification and results rules before each voting period begins.

A digital-first experience

The annual ceremony is designed for worldwide digital participation, with a main show supported by red carpet coverage, live performances, backstage interviews, an afterparty and community watch parties.

The symbol

The Golden Raven with Spectrum Crystal represents intelligence, imagination, individuality and the many colors of human creativity.

Spectrum Awards is in development. Official launch and ceremony dates remain to be announced.
