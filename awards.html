The program

The Awards

One annual celebration. A worldwide field of talent. Recognition across a spectrum of disciplines.

An annual awards season

Each awards year will have its own published program, category list, nomination process, voting period, results certification and ceremony. The launch date and first ceremony date have not yet been announced.

Competitive awards

Competitive categories recognize eligible nominees through public voting. The nominee receiving the highest number of valid votes in a category will be the winner, subject to the published rules and any pre-announced tie procedure.

Special recognition

Separate honors may recognize lifetime achievement, legacy, humanitarian impact, global contribution and Hall of Fame induction. Any special-honor selection process will be published separately and will not be misrepresented as a public-voted competitive award.

The trophy

The signature Spectrum Award is the Golden Raven with Spectrum Crystal, designed as a distinctive symbol of achievement across the full program.

Categories, eligibility requirements and award-season dates will be confirmed before nominations open.
