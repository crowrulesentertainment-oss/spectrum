Voting & Rules

The principles that will guide nominations, voting, verification and official results.

Public voting principle

All competitive Spectrum Awards winners are determined 100% by eligible public voting. No private jury or administrator may substitute a preferred winner for the verified public result.

Planned voter safeguards

  • Account-based voting with verified email.
  • One eligible vote per account per category per voting round.
  • Votes are submitted through a secure server-validated process.
  • Duplicate and automated activity controls.
  • Voting opens and closes according to published dates.
  • Final results are reviewed and certified before publication.
  • Published tie procedures apply if categories finish tied.

Privacy and transparency

Individual voter choices will be protected. Aggregate valid vote totals may be published after results are certified. The platform will explain what account information is collected and how it is used.

Official rules

The detailed eligibility, nomination, campaign, voting, dispute, tie-breaking and results rules must be published before the first voting period opens. This page describes the planned principles and is not a substitute for the final official rules.
